Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Your email is how you log in, and that's all we use it for. Payments run through Stripe, so your card details never reach us. And we don't sell your data to anyone.

Who's behind this

Pinlo is a small tool built for affiliate marketers who post on Pinterest. It's not a big company — it's just us. You can reach us directly at pinlosupport@proton.me and an actual person will respond.

What we collect

Honestly, not much.

Your email — that's the main one. We need it to give you access to the app and send you a sign-in link. We don't use it to spam you.

Payment info — we don't collect this at all. Stripe handles everything. Your card number never passes through our servers.

What you type into the app — product names, niches, audiences. This gets sent to the AI to generate your pin copy, then it's gone. We're not storing a log of everything you've ever typed.

Basic usage data — things like how many pins you've generated, which features you use. Standard stuff that helps us figure out what's working and what isn't.

How we use it

To run the app, send your sign-in link, process your payment, and answer your support emails. That's genuinely it. We're not building ad profiles or selling lists. There's no business model here that requires doing anything creepy with your data.

Who else sees it

Four services touch your data in some way:

  • Stripe — processes your payment. Your billing email and payment details go to them. Their privacy policy is at stripe.com/privacy.
  • Anthropic — the AI that generates your pin copy. When you hit generate, your product inputs go to their API. Their privacy policy is at anthropic.com/privacy.
  • Resend — sends your sign-in code email. Your email address is passed to them to deliver the login code. Their privacy policy is at resend.com/privacy.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the site and stores your account data (email, usage count, subscription status) in their KV and D1 database services. Their privacy policy is at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

Nobody else. We're not passing your email to a marketing platform or sharing anything with data brokers.

Local storage

The app stores your sign-in state and theme preference in your browser's local storage. No tracking cookies, no third-party cookies following you around the web.

How long we keep things

Your account data stays as long as your account does. Your pin history lives in your own browser's local storage — you can clear it anytime. If you want your account deleted, email us and we'll remove everything within 30 days.

Your data is yours

You can ask us what we have on you, ask us to fix something, or ask us to delete it entirely. Just email pinlosupport@proton.me and we'll sort it out.

Security

Everything runs over HTTPS. Payments go through Stripe and never touch our side. We can't promise a perfect system exists — none does — but we're not being careless with your stuff. If something ever goes wrong, email us immediately.

Kids

Pinlo is for adults. If you're under 13, this isn't for you. If we somehow end up with data from someone under 13, email us and we'll delete it right away.

If this changes

We'll update the date at the top when something changes. If it's a big change, we'll email you. Small tweaks won't warrant an email blast.

Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of Canada.

Questions

Email pinlosupport@proton.me. We read every email and respond to everything.
