Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026  ·  Effective: June 2026

Short version: We collect your email to give you access to Pinlo. We use Stripe to handle payments. We don't sell your data to anyone, ever.

1. Who We Are

Pinlo ("we", "our", "us") is a Pinterest pin generation tool for affiliate marketers. Our website is getpinlo.com. You can reach us at pinlosupport@proton.me.

2. What We Collect

Information you give us

  • Email address — required to create your account and send you a sign-in link.
  • Payment information — handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — pages visited, features used, pin generation count. Used to improve the product.
  • Pin inputs — product names, niches, and audiences you type. Used only to generate your pin and are not stored long-term.
  • Browser & device info — browser type, operating system, IP address. Standard server logs.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and operate the Pinlo service
  • To send you your sign-in link and account-related emails
  • To process your subscription payment via Stripe
  • To understand how the product is being used and improve it
  • To respond to support requests

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating Pinlo.

4. Third-Party Services

We use a small number of trusted third-party services:

  • Stripe — payment processing. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment data: stripe.com/privacy
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — powers the AI pin generation. Your product inputs are sent to Anthropic's API to generate content. Anthropic's privacy policy: anthropic.com/privacy
  • Cloudflare — hosting and security. Cloudflare may process request metadata as part of serving the site.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any other third parties.

5. Cookies

We use minimal cookies — only what is necessary to keep you signed in and remember your preferences (such as light/dark mode). We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies.

6. Data Retention

We retain your email and account data for as long as your account is active. Pin history is stored in your browser's local storage and can be cleared by you at any time. If you delete your account, we remove your data within 30 days.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of any non-essential communications

To exercise any of these rights, email us at pinlosupport@proton.me.

8. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data. All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe and never touch our servers. That said, no system is 100% secure — if you believe there has been a security incident, please contact us immediately.

9. Children

Pinlo is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. If we make significant changes, we'll notify you by email. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of Pinlo after changes means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at pinlosupport@proton.me — we'll get back to you within 2 business days.
