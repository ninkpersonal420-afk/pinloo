Key terms explained
Everything you need to know to get the most out of Pinlo.
📈
SEO — Search Engine Optimization
How your content gets found. On Pinterest, it means using the right keywords in your title and description so when someone searches "yoga mat for beginners," your pin shows up — not someone else's. Better SEO = more organic reach, more clicks, more commissions.

🎨
Tone
Tone is set automatically based on your pin goal — urgent for clicks, casual for selling, inspirational for saves and inspire pins. This keeps the copy consistent without extra decisions.
💰
Affiliate Marketing
You get a unique link for each product you promote. When someone clicks your pin and buys through your link, you earn a percentage of the sale — typically 1–10%. No inventory, no customers, no fulfilment. You create the pin, they buy, you get paid.
🏷️
Niche
Your content category. Niche accounts perform better because Pinterest recommends your content to people already interested in that topic. Pinlo uses your niche to write copy that matches what your audience is actually searching for.
⚡
SEO Score
Pinlo scores each pin from 0–100 based on keyword placement, title length, and description quality. 80+ is well-optimized. 60–79 is solid. Below 60 means it could rank better — regenerate and compare. Pro feature.
The story
I built this because
I needed it
Running a Pinterest affiliate business. The traffic side clicked. The copy side never got easier.
A title and description for every single pin, every single day. It sounds small. It isn't. If you're posting consistently across a few niches, you're making dozens of these decisions a week — getting the keywords right, making the hook strong enough to stop a scroll, keeping the description from sounding like a product listing.
I tried ChatGPT. Generic. Didn't understand how Pinterest search works. Templates. Too rigid. Nothing out there was actually built for Pinterest affiliate marketing specifically.
So I built Pinlo. It knows what a Pinterest title needs to do. It knows the description has to lead with a problem, not a product name. You put in what you're promoting and it takes care of the rest.

Common questions
Why did my pin count go down? +
Each time you click Generate, it uses one pin credit. Bulk generation counts each pin individually. Your count is tied to your email and saved on our server — it won't reset if you clear your browser.
How do I upgrade to Pro? +
Go to the Pricing tab and hit the upgrade button. Payment is processed through Stripe and Pro activates instantly after checkout.
My generated pins don't match my niche. +
Make sure you've selected a niche before generating. If auto-detect picked the wrong one, click a different niche pill to override it. You can also add a target audience to guide the output.
