FAQ

Common questions

How is this different from just using ChatGPT? +
ChatGPT works, but you're doing most of the work. You have to explain Pinterest SEO, your niche, the character limits, and the tone you want — every single session. Pinlo already knows all of that. Type the product, get the pin. The output is built around how Pinterest actually ranks content, not just what sounds good to a language model.
How is Pinlo different from other pin generators? +
Most pin tools are full dashboards — templates, brand kits, image editors, schedulers, CSV imports — and you need a tutorial video just to make your first pin. Pinlo does one thing: it writes your pin copy. One field, one button, done in seconds. There's nothing to set up and nothing to learn.
Will Pinterest penalize generated descriptions? +
There's no evidence Pinterest treats AI-written copy differently — its algorithm focuses on keywords, engagement, and freshness. What matters is whether people click and save it. That's exactly what Pinlo's output is built to do.
Can I cancel Pro anytime? +
Yes — cancel from your account settings whenever you want. You keep access until the end of the billing period and nothing gets charged after that.
What affiliate programs does Pinlo work with? +
All of them. Pinlo generates the copy — you add your own link. Amazon Associates, ShareASale, Impact, LTK, whatever you're using — it doesn't matter.
Can I edit the output before I post it? +
Yes — the title and description are fully editable right in the app. Click into them and type. The character counter updates as you go so you know you're still within Pinterest's limits. You can also hit "Regenerate" on any individual field to get a fresh title, description, or hashtag set. Pro users also get an A/B title picker that automatically generates a second title with a different hook after every generation.
